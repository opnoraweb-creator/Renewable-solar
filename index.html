How the Process Works

  1. Check eligibility and electricity connection details.
  2. Complete registration through the official scheme portal.
  3. Choose an eligible vendor and arrange a site survey.
  4. Complete installation as per applicable requirements.
  5. Inspection, net-metering and commissioning process.
  6. Eligible subsidy processing as per government rules.

Scheme eligibility, approval and subsidy are subject to applicable government guidelines and DISCOM procedures.
